# Earth Online

An RPG-style life tracker where your daily actions level up your real-world stats.

## Overview

Earth Online lets you track personal stats (Power, Speed, Intelligence, etc.) day by day. Each day you log actions that raise or lower your stats, confirm the day, and build a history of your progress over time.
